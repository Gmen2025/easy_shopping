Account Deletion for AdduGenet EShop

This page applies to users of the AdduGenet EShop mobile app. It explains how to request deletion of your account and what data is deleted or retained.

Last updated: March 7, 2026

How To Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the AdduGenet EShop app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile and open Edit Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm the deletion request.
  4. If you cannot access the app, email privacy@addugenet.com from your registered email with subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  5. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deletion to protect account security.

Standard processing time is up to 30 days after identity verification.

What Data Is Deleted

Data Type What Happens After Deletion Request
Account profile data Name, email, phone number, address, and profile details are deleted from active systems within 30 days.
Authentication data Login access is disabled immediately, and credentials/tokens are deleted from active systems.
Saved preferences and app data User-specific preferences and stored app settings are removed.

What Data May Be Retained

Data Type Reason and Retention Period
Order and payment transaction records Retained for legal, tax, accounting, anti-fraud, and dispute-resolution obligations for up to 7 years, as required by applicable law.
Security and audit logs May be retained for up to 12 months for security monitoring and abuse prevention.
Backups Encrypted backups may contain residual data for up to 90 days before automatic overwrite.
Data under legal hold Retained only as long as required by law, court order, or active investigation.

Need Help?

For questions about account deletion or privacy rights, contact: privacy@addugenet.com or support@addugenet.com.
