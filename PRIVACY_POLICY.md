# Privacy Policy for AdduGenet EShop

**Last updated: October 23, 2026**

## 1. About this policy

AdduGenet EShop ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AdduGenet EShop application and related services. This policy explains how information is handled for accounts, shopping, deliveries, rides, store and driver services, maintenance requests, and support. Available features and providers depend on your region, account role, app version, and service configuration.

For privacy questions or requests, email [easyshop@addugeneteshop.com](mailto:easyshop@addugeneteshop.com). A business mailing address is not currently published; contact this email for operator details.

This policy is a notice, not blanket consent to every use of information. Where a feature requires permission or consent, we request it separately.

## 2. Information we handle

### Accounts, social sign-in, and security

We handle your name, email, phone number, account identifier, delivery/profile addresses, role, and verification status. For password accounts, the backend stores a password hash rather than the password in readable form.

If enabled and chosen by you, Google, Apple, or Facebook sign-in supplies a provider-specific identifier and permitted profile information, such as your name and email. We process provider tokens to verify sign-in and associate the identity with your account; we do not receive your provider password. A provider may supply a private relay email or limited profile information. The provider also processes your sign-in under its own policy.

Optional two-factor authentication (2FA) uses an authenticator app or verified email, not SMS. We process enrollment settings, verification challenges, expiry and attempt information, and recovery-code records. Authenticator secrets are encrypted on the backend and recovery codes are stored as hashes. Email codes pass through the configured email delivery provider. Keep authenticator setup keys and recovery codes private.

### Shopping, deliveries, and payments

We handle cart and order contents, amounts and currencies, payment method/status and transaction references, refunds, delivery instructions, recipient names and phone numbers, addresses, map coordinates, and fulfillment history. Cross-border orders may include sender/recipient and destination details needed to arrange shipping.

Card details entered through Stripe's payment interface are processed by Stripe; our backend uses payment identifiers and transaction results rather than storing full card numbers or card security codes. Other supported methods, including Telebirr, bank transfers, or cash, involve the contact details, sender names, bank/payment references, and confirmation information needed for that method. Store/driver financial records can include balances, commissions, bank or payout account details, and payout history.

If you provide another person's delivery or contact information, ensure you are authorized to share it and tell them how it will be used.

### Rides, business roles, and service requests

Ride records can include pickup/drop-off locations, intermediate stops, route estimates, seats, shared/regular ride selection, driver assignment, trip progress and timestamps, cancellations, fares, and payment records.

Driver/store applications and operations can include business/contact details, approval decisions, availability, driver photos, vehicle make/model, registration plate, insurance details, store/branch locations, and financial records.

Maintenance/service requests can include equipment details, problem descriptions, photos/videos, service addresses, contact numbers, technician assignments/notes, quotes, and payment status. We also handle reviews and information you send to support. Avoid uploading identity documents, financial secrets, or other sensitive information unless specifically needed.

### Location and uploaded media

With device permission, location features access precise foreground GPS coordinates for pickup/delivery selection, nearby services, route calculation, dispatch, and driver/trip progress. Relevant driver screens can refresh or watch location while in use, and ride location updates are sent to the service. Selected addresses, trip locations, and driver location records can remain on the backend after the screen closes. Current app location features request foreground permission; they are not designed as continuous background tracking.

Manual addresses and selected map points can still be processed when GPS permission is denied. Maps/search/routing providers receive the queries, addresses, or coordinates needed for those requests. Camera/photo-library access is used for media you choose to capture or select, including product, driver, store, and service-request media. Uploaded files may contain personal details or embedded metadata; check them before sharing.

### Notifications, local storage, and technical information

We register push notification tokens to send account, order, ride, delivery, and service updates. App/server and infrastructure logs can include request details, IP addresses, timestamps, errors, and operational activity needed to run and troubleshoot services. This does not mean that every page view, navigation action, or crash is recorded by a dedicated analytics service.

The app uses local storage for sign-in tokens, cart/preferences, and other app state. This storage is not represented as fully encrypted. Protect your device and do not share an unlocked device signed into your account.

## 3. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; verify social sign-in and 2FA; process purchases, payments, refunds, and payouts; dispatch deliveries and rides; fulfill service requests; administer store/driver participation; send operational updates; answer support requests; diagnose failures; prevent abuse; and manage disputes and applicable legal obligations.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, the basis depends on the use and jurisdiction: providing a requested service or contract, complying with a legal obligation, legitimate interests such as service security where permitted and balanced against your rights, or consent where required. Device permission does not authorize unrelated uses of your information.

## 4. Who receives information

Relevant information is shared with the people fulfilling your request, such as stores, delivery drivers, ride drivers, technicians, and authorized support/administration staff. This can include recipient/contact details, addresses, order or service details, and trip information. Customers can see relevant assigned-driver identity, vehicle, and location/progress information. On shared rides, other passengers may observe shared stops, routes, and fellow passengers; do not assume your journey is private from other occupants.

Service providers process information needed for their functions. Depending on configuration, these include Render (hosting), MongoDB (database services), Cloudinary (uploaded media), Expo and platform push services (notifications), Google Maps/Places/Routes (maps and routing), Stripe and Telebirr (payments), Google/Apple/Facebook (chosen social sign-in), and SMTP or Resend (email). Listing a provider does not mean that every user uses every integration.

Provider policies include [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Meta/Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/), [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy), [Expo](https://expo.dev/privacy), [Cloudinary](https://cloudinary.com/privacy), [Render](https://render.com/privacy), [MongoDB](https://www.mongodb.com/legal/privacy-policy), and [Resend](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy). For Telebirr, consult the notice in the payment service you use.

Opening external maps, email, telephone, Telegram, or WhatsApp transfers you to that application or service. Your communications and information shared there are also subject to that provider's policies. Reviews and public business/product content may be visible to other users.

We may disclose relevant records when required by applicable law, to respond to lawful requests, or as necessary to protect people, investigate fraud, and resolve disputes. We do not describe operational sharing with payment, delivery, or hosting services as anonymous.

## 5. Your permissions and choices

You can edit available profile details, manage optional 2FA in Security Settings, and control location, camera/photos, and notification permissions in device settings. Denying a permission can limit the related feature. Disabling notifications does not cancel an order or prevent necessary in-app/email communications.

You can choose password sign-in instead of social sign-in where available. Provider permissions can be managed with Google, Apple, or Facebook. Disconnecting a provider is not the same as deleting your EShop account or previously collected records.

Signing out ends the app's local signed-in session; it is not account deletion or a guarantee that every server token is immediately revoked. Uninstalling the app does not delete server records. Device settings can clear local app data, but this does not remove copies already uploaded.

## 6. Retention

There are no established, universal fixed retention periods for all record categories. We determine retention based on why information is needed, ongoing transactions/services, security, disputes, applicable legal/accounting requirements, and the capabilities of the systems holding it. We do not promise blanket 30-day deletion, 90-day backup removal, or seven-year transaction retention.

Account, order, ride, driver/store, service, uploaded-media, authentication, and provider records can exist in separate systems. Temporary authentication challenges have expiry controls, but expiry does not establish a deletion deadline for every related record. Backups, logs, and third-party copies can follow different retention arrangements. Contact us for a review of a specific record or request.

## 7. Account deletion and other privacy requests

In the app, go to **Profile > Edit Profile > Delete Account** and confirm. On a successful response, the current implementation removes the account's user record and signs you out locally. This is not automatic deletion of all related data: orders, payments, rides, driver/store profiles, service requests, media, separate social-identity/authentication records, and backups may remain. Previously issued tokens are not guaranteed to be invalidated immediately by this action.

For a broader deletion request, or if you cannot sign in, email [easyshop@addugeneteshop.com](mailto:easyshop@addugeneteshop.com) with subject **Account Deletion Request**. Include your registered email and the scope of your request. We may verify ownership; never send passwords, one-time codes, authenticator keys, recovery codes, or full payment card details.

We review requests and explain applicable retention needs or technical limitations. We have not established a general guaranteed completion deadline; legally required response periods still apply where applicable. Deleting your account does not itself cancel pending deliveries/rides, settle outstanding payments, or remove records held independently by payment/social providers or people who already received them.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, a copy/portability, restriction or objection to certain processing, withdrawal of consent, or applicable sale/sharing opt-outs. These rights can have exceptions. You may also complain to your competent data protection authority. See the [account deletion instructions](account-deletion.html).

## 8. Security

Safeguards include password hashing, access checks, expiring authentication challenges, encrypted authenticator secrets, and hashed recovery codes. Protection also depends on deployment configuration, provider security, and your device/account practices. No transmission or storage system is completely secure; we do not promise that all stored information is encrypted or that every deployment has been independently audited.

Production connections should use HTTPS. Do not send sensitive information through an untrusted connection. Report suspected account misuse to our contact email without sending credentials.

## 9. International processing

The service supports activities in Ethiopia and the USA, including cross-border fulfillment. Hosting, storage, payment, authentication, maps, communications, and support providers may process information in other countries with different privacy protections. Applicable transfer requirements depend on the parties, locations, and law; this policy does not claim a particular certification or transfer agreement is in place for every provider.

## 10. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. Adult authorization is required where applicable, including for minors entering transactions. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address the information. This notice does not mean the app performs age verification.
